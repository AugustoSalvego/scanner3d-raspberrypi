Calibrate — 9 × 6 Checkerboard

One upright checkerboard pose, real camera, rotation axis and laser plane. All three are required for accurate 3D reconstruction.

Before starting: secure your rigid, flat checkerboard on the turntable, approximately 2 cm off-centre, but without tilting the platform. Measure a printed square with a ruler. The camera must stay fixed during both calibration and scanning. Never look directly into the laser.
